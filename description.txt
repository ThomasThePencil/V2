Voraria: Second Course, a long-term successor to and reimagining of the original Voraria mod by Maroy, is a mod which is best described as 3 mods in a trenchcoat:
1) A vore mod, intended to add vore to just about everything in the game, sans some obvious exceptions.
2) A content mod, intended to add large swathes of new vorish and engaging content to the game.
3) An overhaul mod, intended to remake large portions of the game to be more suitable to add vore to and design vore around.
